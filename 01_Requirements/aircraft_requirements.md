
# Aircraft Design Requirements

**Project:** Fixed-Wing RC Aircraft  
**Author:** Elias Hayek  
**Document ID:** REQ-001  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Status:** Preliminary

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the initial engineering requirements for the design, construction, and experimental validation of a manually controlled fixed-wing RC aircraft.

Requirements may be revised as aerodynamic, structural, and experimental data become available.

## 2. Mission Profile

The aircraft is intended for recreational manual flight under calm weather conditions.

The operating concept includes:

- Hand launching
- Stable and maneuverable flight
- Manual radio control
- Belly landing
- Repeated flight testing
- Iterative engineering improvements

## 5. Structural Requirements

STR-01: The structure shall withstand expected flight and landing loads.

STR-02: The wing attachment system shall permit removal and reinstallation.

STR-03: The fuselage shall accommodate the propulsion system, battery, receiver, and servos.

STR-04: The aircraft shall allow access to electronic components for inspection and maintenance.

STR-05: Structural integrity shall be inspected before each flight.

Structural load factors, allowable stresses, and safety margins will be defined during detailed design.

## 6. Stability and Control Requirements

CTRL-01: The aircraft shall demonstrate controllable pitch, roll, and yaw behavior.

CTRL-02: The center of gravity shall remain within a verified operating range.

CTRL-03: The control surfaces shall provide sufficient authority for the intended flight envelope.

CTRL-04: Control-system functionality shall be verified before flight.

## 7. Manufacturing Requirements

MFG-01: The aircraft shall be manufacturable using accessible tools and materials.

MFG-02: Critical structural components shall be inspectable.

MFG-03: Major components shall be replaceable or repairable where practical.

## 8. Verification Strategy

Requirements will be verified through:

- Engineering calculations
- Aerodynamic simulations
- CAD and structural analysis
- Ground testing
- Flight testing
- Experimental data comparison

Each requirement will be marked as:

- Not Started
- In Progress
- Verified
- Failed / Requires Modification
