# Mass Budget

| Case | Mass | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V2 sizing case | 630 g | Chosen design mass |
| Detailed build estimate | 616.07 g | Includes temporary ESP32 and remaining build allowance |
| Estimated flight configuration | 596.07 g | Detailed estimate less 20 g for the ESP32 |
| Margin over detailed estimate | 13.93 g | 630 − 616.07 g |

The detailed estimate includes electronics and hardware, propeller, wood and plywood structure, and an 85 g remaining build allowance. That allowance covers glue, covering, additional sheeting, leading- and trailing-edge stock, reinforcements, mounts, Velcro, tape and minor hardware.

| Item | Mass | Basis |

| Propeller | 9.7 g | Component mass from handoff |
| Battery | 83.6 g | Component mass from handoff |
| ESC with BEC | 20 g | Approximate |
| Four SG90 servos | 36 g | 4 × 9 g |
| ESP32 | 20 g | Temporary allowance |
| Receiver and gyroscope | Included in component list | Individual masses not supplied in handoff |

The 630 g sizing case carries margin while the airframe estimate is refined. More mass raises wing loading and the lift required at 6 m/s. The itemized V2 workbook and component stations are needed to audit the full sum and reproduce the CG result.
