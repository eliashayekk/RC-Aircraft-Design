| --- | --- |

| Tractor propeller | 10 × 4.5 in (1045), 254 mm diameter, 114.3 mm nominal pitch, approximately 9.7 g |

| ESC | Approximately 20 g, BEC included |

The hardware combination defines the propulsion case to analyze. Record the exact motor model, ESC rating and battery capacity before assessing current draw, thrust, electrical margins and endurance. A different propeller or battery changes motor load, available thrust and potentially the mass and CG.
