# 3D Wing

Start with an isolated rectangular NACA 4412 wing in XFLR5. Use 1.55 m span, 238 mm constant chord, 0.3689 m² reference area, and zero sweep, twist and dihedral. Document the method, panel settings, viscous polar coverage and convergence. Run NACA 2412 with the same geometry and settings when comparing the candidates.

1. Sweep angle of attack across the relevant speeds and inspect the spanwise loading, lift curve and induced drag.
2. Identify where the wing first approaches local section lift limits. Record solver or polar coverage limits when interpreting a near-stall result.
3. Use a supported finite-wing maximum lift estimate to assess the 6 m/s target. A fixed percentage reduction from section lift or a linear inviscid polar alone does not establish stall.
4. Use the supported wing results to narrow launch and cruise conditions. Add the tails for trim, incidence, neutral-point and static-margin analysis before confirming the CG and launch condition.

Keeping the wing geometry fixed isolates the airfoil's effect. A later change to twist, span or chord would change the distribution of lift and the stall progression.
