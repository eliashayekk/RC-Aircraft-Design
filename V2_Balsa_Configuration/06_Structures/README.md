# Structures

The balsa wing concept uses ribs, a main spar, a rear spar, leading-edge stock and sheeting, and a reinforced center mounting region for the removable wing. The mounting concept uses two front locating dowels and two rear M4 nylon bolts with approximately 40 mm spacing provisionally recorded. Verify spacing and fastener layout in CAD. Use plywood or hardwood reinforcement at concentrated loads.

NACA 4412 gives a nominal maximum section thickness of 28.6 mm at a 238 mm chord. NACA 4415 gives 35.7 mm at the same chord, which offers more potential spar depth and changes the aerodynamic trade-off. The available spar depth depends on its chordwise station and the rib and sheeting geometry.

Finalize the airfoil before detailing rib cutouts, spar position and depth, sheeting and the attachment load path.
