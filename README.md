**Author:** Elias Hayek  

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on the design, analysis, manufacturing, and experimental validation of a fixed-wing radio-controlled aircraft.

The objective is to apply fundamental aerospace engineering principles through a complete design cycle, progressing from initial requirements and aerodynamic analysis to physical prototyping, flight testing, and iterative improvement.

The aircraft is intended for manual recreational flight, with an emphasis on stability, maneuverability, structural simplicity, and practical manufacturability.

## 2. Design Objectives

- Achieve stable and controllable manual flight.
- Target a stall speed of approximately 6 m/s.
- Support practical hand launching and belly landing.
- Balance maneuverability with predictable handling.
- Investigate the feasibility of 30-minute endurance.
- Design a removable wing for easier transportation and maintenance.
- Validate design assumptions through experimental testing.

## 4. Engineering Methodology

The project follows an iterative engineering design process:

1. Define aircraft requirements and constraints.
2. Perform preliminary aircraft sizing.
3. Analyze airfoil performance and wing aerodynamics.
4. Evaluate longitudinal stability and control.
5. Size the propulsion and electrical systems.
6. Develop CAD models and structural components.
7. Manufacture and assemble the aircraft.
8. Conduct ground and flight tests.
9. Analyze experimental results and improve the design.

## 5. Aerodynamic Analysis

XFLR5 is used to investigate:

- Airfoil lift and drag characteristics
- Reynolds number effects
- Wing aerodynamic performance
- Aircraft stability characteristics
- Tail incidence and pitching moment behavior

Python is used to process exported results, compare configurations, and generate engineering plots.

Detailed calculations and results will be documented in the aerodynamic analysis directory.

## 6. CAD and Structural Design

SolidWorks is used to develop the aircraft geometry and prepare the design for manufacturing.

The CAD work includes the fuselage, wing, tail surfaces, and supporting structural components.

The design will account for component placement, accessibility, manufacturing methods, and assembly.

## 7. Manufacturing and Experimental Testing

The aircraft will be physically constructed and evaluated through ground and flight testing.

Testing will focus on:

- Structural integrity
- Center of gravity and balance
- Control-surface operation
- Propulsion performance
- Flight stability
- Handling and maneuverability
- Endurance

Experimental observations will guide subsequent modifications.

**Current status:** Manufacturing and flight testing are planned. No flight-test results have been reported yet.
